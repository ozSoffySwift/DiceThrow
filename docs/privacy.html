Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 August 2026 · applies to Dice Throw for iPhone

Dice Throw does not ask for an account, and never collects your name, email, contacts, location, photos, or any other personal information.

What stays on your device

  • Your dice pool and every throw in your History are stored only on your device, using Apple's SwiftData framework. They are never uploaded anywhere.
  • Your settings — sound, haptics, shake-to-throw and shake sensitivity — stay on your device.

What is collected

Since version 1.1 the app uses Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the app is used, so it can be improved:

  • Usage events — that a throw happened and how it was started (tap or shake), the dice in your pool, the total rolled, how long the dice took to settle, which screens you opened, and when you change a setting.
  • A device identifier — an anonymous per-install ID generated by Firebase, plus standard device information (model, OS version, country, language).
  • Diagnostics — basic crash and performance data.

The app deliberately uses the Firebase Analytics variant that has no access to the advertising identifier (IDFA). It does not track you across other apps or websites, contains no advertising, and shares no data with advertisers or data brokers. This is why the app does not show an App Tracking Transparency prompt.

Analytics data is processed by Google on our behalf. See Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses data from apps that use its services.

Children

The app is rated 4+ and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.
