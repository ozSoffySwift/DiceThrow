Version 1.1

• New d12. The twelve-sided die joins the pool, with the same beveled edges and
  real material finish as the rest of the set.

• Faster results. The dice settle and the total appears roughly twice as fast —
  no more waiting after the dice have obviously stopped.

• Correct d6 pips. The dots on the six-sided dice now sit where they do on a
  real die. The 3, 4 and 6 faces were noticeably off before.

• Clearer shadows. Dice cast a stronger, sharper shadow, so you can tell at a
  glance how high one is in the air as it falls.
