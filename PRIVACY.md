# Privacy Policy

**Dice Throw** does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data.

- The app has no network access, no analytics, no third-party SDKs, and no advertising.
- Throw history is stored only on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework, and is
  never sent anywhere.
- The app does not require or support account creation, and does not access contacts,
  location, camera, microphone, or any other sensitive device data.

If you have questions about this policy, contact ozsoffy@gmail.com.
